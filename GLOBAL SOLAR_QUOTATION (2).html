1Project Overview

Development of a private Android Client Management App for Global Solar 2.0 to manage:

  • Client / Lead management (Talaja, Bhavnagar & nearby villages)
  • Follow-ups & reminders
  • Quotes based on Adani TOPCon package pricing
  • Payments & documents (₹10,000 registration, balance, Aadhaar / Electricity Bill / Cancelled Cheque)
  • Installation stage tracking (Fitting → PGVCL Inspection → Net Meter → Subsidy)
  • Reports & analytics dashboard
  • Access restricted to only 2 admin users (Founder & Co-Founder) — not a public app
