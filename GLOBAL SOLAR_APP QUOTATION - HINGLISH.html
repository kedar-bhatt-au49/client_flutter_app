1Project Overview (Project kya hai)

Global Solar 2.0 ke liye ek private Android Client Management App banaya ja raha hai, jisse aap yeh sab manage kar sakenge:

  • Client / Lead management (Talaja, Bhavnagar & aas-paas ke gaon)
  • Follow-ups & reminders — kaun kab call karna hai
  • Quotes — Adani TOPCon package pricing ke hisaab se
  • Payments & documents (₹10,000 registration, balance, Aadhaar / Electricity Bill / Cancelled Cheque)
  • Installation stage tracking (Fitting → PGVCL Inspection → Net Meter → Subsidy)
  • Reports & analytics dashboard
  • Access sirf 2 admin users ke liye (Founder & Co-Founder) — yeh public app nahi hai
